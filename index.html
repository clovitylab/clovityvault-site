About us

A quiet bridge between Seoul and the region.

Based in Seoul, Clovity Vault is an export and distribution partner for Korean beauty brands entering Southeast Asia.

We work with a curated portfolio of independent brands and take their products from Korean shelves to customers in Singapore, Malaysia and beyond, bringing the regulatory, logistics and marketplace expertise each market requires.

What we do

From the brand's shelf to the customer's hands.

One partner for every step of market entry.

01

Sourcing & Quality

Authentic products purchased directly from brands and authorised Korean distributors, inspected and documented before export.

02

Market Entry

Product notification, labelling and compliance support for each market, including Singapore HSA and Malaysia NPRA requirements.

03

Marketplace Operations

Listing, pricing, fulfilment and customer care on Shopee and leading regional platforms, with regular performance reporting for every brand.

The route

From Seoul to Southeast Asia.

Singapore is our first market, with Malaysia and Taiwan next. Every shipment leaves Seoul fully documented and arrives ready for local sale.

Our standards

Our commitment to every brand and customer.

i.

Genuine products only

Every product is purchased from the brand or an authorised Korean distributor. We do not trade in grey-market stock.

ii.

Full traceability

Supplier, batch and shipment records are kept for every product we sell and shared with brand partners on request.

iii.

Local compliance

We follow the cosmetic regulations and marketplace policies of every market we operate in.

Care in every step

Chosen for how it feels, not only how it sells.

We select formulas and brands we are proud to stand behind, and handle every order with the same care.

브랜드 파트너

한국 브랜드의 동남아 첫 판매, 작게 시작해 함께 키웁니다.

클로비티볼트는 서울에 있는 화장품 수출·유통 회사입니다. 싱가포르를 시작으로 말레이시아·대만으로 넓혀 가며, 아직 동남아에 공식 판매처가 없는 한국 브랜드와 함께합니다.

01

판매 수권

쇼피 싱가포르 비독점 공식 판매자로 시작합니다. 큰 계약이나 독점을 먼저 요구하지 않습니다.

02

싱가포르 신고

싱가포르 화장품 신고(HSA)와 현지 책임 명의를 저희가 준비합니다. 브랜드는 전성분·제조 정보만 주시면 됩니다.

03

정품 판매 운영

브랜드 또는 공식 유통사에서 받은 정품만 판매하고, 상품 등록·가격·배송·고객 응대를 맡습니다.

04

분기 성적표

판매 수량·리뷰·현지 검색량을 분기마다 한 장으로 공유합니다. 성과가 쌓이면 지역 공식 유통을 함께 논의합니다.
