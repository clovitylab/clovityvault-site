About us

A quiet bridge between Seoul and the region.

Clovity Vault is a Seoul-based company that sources Korean beauty products and brings them to customers in Southeast Asia through online marketplaces.

We focus on quality indie brands and aim to grow together with them — one considered product, one market at a time.

What we do

From the brand's shelf to the customer's hands.

Three things, done with care.

Sourcing

We purchase authentic products directly from brands and authorized Korean suppliers.

02

Cross-border sales

03

Helping Korean indie brands enter Southeast Asian markets, step by step.

The route

From Seoul to Singapore.

We start in Singapore and plan to expand to Malaysia and Taiwan — bringing products from Korea to the region through trusted online marketplaces.

Our standards

What we promise to every brand and customer.

i.

Genuine products only

We are committed to buying authentic products from brands and authorized Korean suppliers.

ii.

Transparent sourcing

We aim to keep a clear record of where each product comes from, and to be open about it with the brands we work with.

iii.

Respect for local rules

We are committed to following the product and marketplace regulations of each market we sell in.

Care in every step

Chosen for how it feels, not only how it sells.

We look for formulas and brands we would be proud to put our name next to — and we handle every order with the same care.
